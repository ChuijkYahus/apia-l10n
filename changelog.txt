Version: 0.5.0
Date: 30-09-2026
  Major Features:
    - Apia planet was reworked.
  Features:
    - Artificial hives now produce 3 types of combs: honey, iron, and copper.
    - Fossils now process only into stone and phosphorus.
    - Copper and iron ore can be obtained from copper and iron honeycombs.
    - New natural soil types have been added: Honey lichen, Lichen with copper coating, and Lichen with iron coating.
    - New artificial soils have been added: honey, copper, and iron lichen.
    - Added a setting for the amount of fertile soil in Apia.
    - An artificial hive can now only be placed on honey, iron, or copper lichen.
    - The phosphorus texture has become redder.
    - A new "tips and tricks" entry for Apia honeycombs production has been added.
    - The iron and copper bacteria of the planet Gleba can now be refreshed.
  Graphics:
    - Bio solar panel icon have been retextured.
    - Artificial hive have been retextured.
  Balancing:
    - Alternative recipes of carbon fiber for Apia and Carnova have been removed. Gleba would have turned out to be useless if they had stayed.
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed factoriopedia links for Apia and Carnova tiles.
